Multi-Asset Portfolio Tracker

Track Indian equities, mutual funds, US stocks, LRS limits, and watchlist theses via Demo Mode or live Google Sheets sync.

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-currency INR (₹) and USD ($) switching with USD/INR reference tracking
  • Asset allocation across Indian equities, mutual funds, US stocks, and cash reserves
  • RBI Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) annual utilization monitor
  • Holdings table, deployment watchlist pipeline, thesis notes, and retirement roadmap

Privacy & Data Sources

Runs in Demo Mode completely offline in your browser with sample multi-asset data, or connects to your private Google Apps Script / Cloudflare Worker JSON endpoint. No brokerage credentials are requested.
